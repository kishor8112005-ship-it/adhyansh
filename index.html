01 — The room

Not a showroom.
A working gym.

Kandivali opened in 2020, in the worst possible year to open a gym. It survived on one idea: a room where beginners get coached, regulars get left alone, and nobody is selling you something at the door.

01

Iron floor with old-school bones

Racks, benches, plate-loaded machines and a dumbbell run that goes the distance. Members call the basement “old-school vibes” — we take that as a compliment.

02

Cardio with a view

Treadmills, ellipticals and cycles lined up facing out. Come for the sunset over the skyline; stay because the 24×7 door means your session never gets cut short.

03

Coaching that starts on day one

Every new member gets a floor walkthrough, a form check and a starter split. Want closer eyes? Personal training slots run through the night too.

04

A recovery wing that earns its keep

Steam, shower and secure lockers under the same roof — so you can walk in from work and walk out ready for the rest of your day.

11 — Questions

Before you
walk in.

Still unsure about something? The front desk replies on WhatsApp, usually within minutes.

Ask on WhatsApp
What are your timings?

Kandivali is open 24 hours, seven days a week — including public holidays. The front desk is staffed round the clock, and coaches are on the floor through all shifts.

How do I book a free trial?

Use the form below or WhatsApp +91 93243 37728. Tell us a day and a time window; we’ll hold it. Bring shoes, a water bottle and an ID for the locker.

What’s included in a membership?

Floor access (strength, cardio, functional), floor-coach guidance, lockers, showers and steam. Spinning, nutrition consultations and personal training are added depending on the plan.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No. Bars, plates, dumbbells, kettlebells, ropes and cardio machines are all here. Bring training shoes, a towel and a bottle — chalk is on the house.

Are there different membership plans?

Yes — day pass, monthly, quarterly and annual, plus personal training bundles. Couple, student and renewal rates are available at the desk.

Can I transfer my membership to another branch?

Yes. Transfers are allowed between Adiyash branches, and the annual plan includes all-branch access so you can train at any of the seven locations.

I’m a complete beginner. Will I feel out of place?

That’s most of the room. Your first session includes a walkthrough and a starter plan, and the floor coaches will set up your first few exercises with you.
